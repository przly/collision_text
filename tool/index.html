Controls

A quick guide to what everything in the panel does.

1. Edit

Click the pink Edit Text & Image button to open the panel and set your own text, images, and other parameters.

2. Export

Click the black Export Video button, pick a duration, allow the tab recording permission, then wait for the video to render.

Words & Accent

Type up to three headlines and two accent words (e.g. a date, starting time).

Accents render as the pink-coloured text in the animation.

Press Enter in any inputfield to break it onto a new line.

Font Size scales each headline and accent group independently, and the trash buttons clears a field.

Image 1 & Image 2

Choose an image or drag one onto the upload area in the control panel.

Rectangle keeps the image's original aspect ratio, while Square forces a 1:1 ratio.

Size and Corner Radius adjust its scale and corner rounding, and Rotation tilts the images. Each image can be rotated and scaled independently.

Export Video

Choose a duration for the exported clip.
